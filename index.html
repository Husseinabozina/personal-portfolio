02 / A little about me

Good interfaces.
Dependable foundations.

I’m Hussein, a Mobile Software Engineer. Flutter/Dart is my strongest specialization: 5 years of hands-on development and work across 20+ applications, including production apps, company and client work, and independent engineering projects.

My native work includes Mahami: separate Swift/SwiftUI iOS and Kotlin/Jetpack Compose Android applications, with persistent local data, native reminders and optional personal sync. This complements my production Flutter background. I’ve also owned the custom backend on an end-to-end project.

I use AI-Augmented Engineering throughout delivery: structure requirements and context, define acceptance criteria, anticipate implementation mistakes, review generated code, iterate and verify the result. Responsibility for the final implementation stays with me.
